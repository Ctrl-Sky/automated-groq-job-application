About the job
Position Overview:

We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented student to join our Technical Services team for the summer term. In this role, you will lead the development and implementation of interactive reporting solutions using Power BI. Your primary objective will be to design and build a comprehensive dashboard ecosystem that tracks the end-to-end flow of material—from mine production through processing to final concentrate.
Working closely with operations and technical teams, you will consolidate disparate industrial data streams into a streamlined, accessible, and scalable reporting framework, helping establish the foundation for long-term data architecture improvements.


Responsibilities:

Dashboard Development: Design, develop, and deploy interactive Power BI dashboards that provide clear, near real-time visibility into material flow from mine to final concentrate. 
Data Integration: Connect, transform, and model data from multiple operational systems, including: 
Live process plant data (PI Historian) 
Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS) 
Mine production and fleet data (shift-based) 
Data Architecture Support: Develop efficient data models using Power Query and DAX, supporting the transition from legacy spreadsheet-based reporting to a centralized, relational database environment. 
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with metallurgists, plant operators, and mine engineers to define key performance indicators (KPIs) and ensure dashboards accurately reflect operational realities. 
Documentation: Produce clear technical documentation outlining data connections, transformation logic, and user guides to support long-term maintainability and usability.
Qualifications:

Education: Currently pursuing a degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Mining Engineering, Metallurgical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, or a related technical discipline. 
Technical Skills: 
Strong proficiency in Microsoft Power BI, including Power Query and DAX
Experience with SQL, relational databases, and data modeling 
Familiarity with ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes and data structures 
Industry Knowledge: 
Previous exposure to mining, manufacturing, or industrial environments is highly preferred 
Experience with time-series data systems (e.g., PI Historian) and LIMS is considered an asset 
Soft Skills: 
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities 
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate operational needs into technical solutions 
Ability to work both independently and collaboratively in a team environment
