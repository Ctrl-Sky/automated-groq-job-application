About the job
Capstone Infrastructure Corporation is seeking an AI Enablement Intern to join our IT team. This role will report to the IT System Administrator and is a newly created position. This is a hybrid full-time position as a summer (May 2026 to August 2026) internship with a compensation of $25 per hour. 



In this role, you will help teams across Capstone adopt AI tools safely and effectively. You’ll create training materials, run automation pilots, and build a clear picture of how AI is being used across the organization. 



Key Responsibilities will include, but will not be limited to: 

User Education & Enablement

Help employees use AI tools confidently in their day-to-day work
Create quick-start guides, custom agents, and how-to videos for common use cases.
Run lunch-and-learns / office hours for different skill levels
Collect feedback and track adoption signals
Exploration of other AI tools available within the market 


Automation Discovery

Identify and prioritize automation opportunities across the organization.
Conduct a short AI/automation inventory across departments
Map where AI fits into existing workflows and potential automation pathways
Develop an opportunity backlog/Gantt chart to track problem statements, impacted users, risk level, candidate tool(s), and recommended next step


Data Tagging

Help organize content within SharePoint so that AI tools can better identify data
Propose a tagging/light taxonomy for SharePoint work products
Recommend storage/retention patterns that improve retrieval and reduce duplication. 


Policy & Guardrails for AI Adoption

You will help operationalize common-sense guardrails using existing policies, vendor constraints, and regulatory guidelines 
Work with senior management to develop and revamp existing policies to evolve with AI Adoption while reducing risk surface and maintaining agile workflows 


Please be aware that additional duties may be assigned as needed. 



Qualifications and Experience: 

An ideal candidate would satisfy the following: 

Currently enrolled in (or recently graduated from) a program in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business Analytics, or similar.
Strong communication skills: can teach, write how-to, and work with various stakeholders
Hands-on comfort with modern productivity tools (primarily Microsoft 365)
Demonstrated interest in AI tools and workflow improvement (GenAI, automation, agents).
Hands-on comfort with modern programming tools (PowerShell, Python, Power Automate)
Highly organized, initiative-taking, and meticulous.
Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities under tight timelines.
Positive, professional, and team-oriented attitude.
